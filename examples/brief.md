# Example founder brief — replace with your own

**User and decision:** A local event organizer must decide whether to add overflow seating before a fixed event.

**Current workaround:** Informal messages and a spreadsheet. This is a founder hypothesis, not verified field research.

**Market question:** Will at least 120 checked-in attendees be recorded for the 14 November 2026 sample event by 20:00 Asia/Almaty time?

**Resolution rule:** YES if the organizer’s final check-in export records at least 120 distinct checked-in attendees by 20:00 Asia/Almaty time on 14 November 2026; otherwise NO, if that final export is available and internally consistent.

**VOID policy:** VOID if the event is cancelled, the final check-in export is unavailable, or the export is materially inconsistent such that the threshold cannot be determined.

**Decision moment:** Seven days before the event.

**What a forecast could change:** Whether to reserve overflow seating.

**Evidence supplied:** None. All demand and operational claims above are illustrative assumptions.

**Limits:** This exercise uses a local probability forecast and manual resolution. It does not place trades, custody funds, or attest the real-world event. The export authority and dispute procedure are intentionally unresolved and must be fixed for a real contract.
