# Specify Market

Preserve the brief’s question, resolution rule, and VOID policy word for word. Identify event, comparator, units, time window, evidence source, close condition, resolution authority, dispute handling, and finality. Mark missing choices unresolved. The workshop prototype uses YES/NO/VOID and off-chain simulation. Score a YES forecast p with (p−1)^2, a NO forecast with p^2; VOID has no score. Test 0%, 100%, boundaries, invalid values, closed state, and double resolution.

## Handoff
Write concrete findings and the smallest next verification. Keep simulation, live provider, chain, deployment, and participant outcomes at distinct evidence levels.
