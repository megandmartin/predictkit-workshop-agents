# Collect Evidence

For each material claim, record its source location, what it supports, what remains unknown, and one reproducible next check. A founder assertion is not independent validation; a URL without visited content is only a lead. Distinguish proposed, source-inspected, locally tested, browser-observed, provider-observed, chain-read-back, deployed, and user-measured evidence. Do not fabricate quotes or results.

## Handoff
Write concrete findings and the smallest next verification. Keep simulation, live provider, chain, deployment, and participant outcomes at distinct evidence levels.
